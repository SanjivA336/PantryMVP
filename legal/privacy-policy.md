# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Burrow ("we," "us," "our")
collects, how it's used, and your choices, when you use the Burrow
household pantry-tracking service (the "Service"), operated by
[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME].

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information**: your email address and password (handled by
  our authentication provider, Supabase, which never lets us see or store
  your raw password).
- **Household data you or your household enter**: inventory items, food
  names/quantities/costs, storage locations, shopping lists, recipes,
  usage/consumption logs, and balances between household members.
- **Activity data**: a log of actions taken in your household (who added,
  used, or removed what, and when), shown to household members as an
  activity feed.
- **Receipt photos** (only if you use receipt scanning): uploaded images
  are processed to extract line items and stored so you can review them.
- **Basic technical data**: standard server logs (e.g. IP address,
  timestamps) generated by normal web traffic, and, if enabled, error
  reports (see "Error tracking" below).

We do not collect payment information, since Burrow does not process
payments (see the Terms of Service).

## 2. How we use it

Solely to operate the Service: to show your household's data to you and
the other members of your household, to compute suggested balances and
warnings, and to keep the app working. We do not sell your personal
information, and we do not use third-party advertising or behavioral
tracking of any kind.

## 3. Sharing within a household

Burrow is a multi-person tool by design. Data you add to a household
(inventory, activity, balances, etc.) is visible to every other member of
that household. This is not a bug or a third-party disclosure; it's how
the Service works. Don't add someone to a household whose data you don't
want them to see.

## 4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that make Burrow work, each
acting on our behalf:

- **Supabase**: authentication, database, and file storage.
- **Google Cloud Vision**: receipt-photo text extraction (only for the
  receipt-scanning feature).
- **A locally-run AI model (Ollama)**: recipe parsing/generation
  assistance; this runs on our own infrastructure, not a third party.
- **Resend**: delivering transactional email (e.g. password reset), once
  configured.
- **Sentry**: error tracking, once configured (see below).

We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share your personal information with
third parties.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

**Burrow does not use cookies.** The app stores a small amount of data in
your browser's local storage: your login session (so you stay signed in)
and a couple of display preferences (like your last-used view mode).
This is strictly necessary for the Service to function and is not used
for tracking or advertising, so it doesn't require separate consent under
applicable cookie/ePrivacy laws.

## 6. Error tracking

If enabled, we use Sentry to capture unexpected application errors so we
can fix bugs. Error reports may include technical details about what
happened (e.g. a stack trace, the page you were on) but are not used for
tracking or advertising.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Account settings. Deleting
your account removes your membership and personal data as described at
deletion time; data shared within a household you belonged to (e.g. items
you added) may remain as part of that household's shared history unless
you also remove it beforehand, similar to how a shared document behaves
when one collaborator leaves.

## 10. Security

transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute
